The House

Founded on craft,
run on discipline.

Every student begins at the toile — draping muslin before they ever touch fine cloth. Small cohorts, hands-on tuition, and a workroom open six days a week mean every garment that leaves Couturier Yetty has been considered from the seam up.

We take on private clients alongside our students, so the same standards that govern a graduating collection govern a client's evening gown.
